    Questions

    Before you ask

    What society offices and congress organisers usually want to know first.

    Couldn’t we just build it ourselves with AI?

    You can generate a good-looking page in an afternoon. The work on a meeting site is everything after that: getting dozens of faculty names, titles and affiliations right, publishing the programme change that arrives two days before the meeting, checking that registration links, deadlines and rates are correct, and having one person who answers for it when something is wrong. I use AI tools myself where they save time, which keeps the price down. What you pay for is accuracy, fast updates and someone responsible.

    Is it GDPR-compliant?

    The meeting site itself collects no personal data: registration and payments stay on your existing platform. Fonts are served from the site itself rather than from third parties, and each site has a privacy notice. If you want visitor statistics, they are set up without tracking cookies.

    Where is the site hosted, and who owns it?

    Hosting is agreed with you at the start: on your society’s own servers, or on hosting I set up and manage for you. Either way the domain and the content are yours, and you get a full copy of the site when the meeting is over.

    Is it accessible?

    Sites are built with readable contrast, keyboard navigation, text alternatives for images and layouts that work on phones, which is where most delegates check the programme.

    Does it work with our registration or abstract platform?

    Yes. The site links to or embeds the system you already use, whether that is your society’s own portal or one run by your congress organiser, so nothing changes for your team or your delegates.

    Do you work with PCOs?

    Yes. I can work directly for the society or alongside your professional congress organiser, with one point of contact for approvals.
