Websites for medical society meetings

I build standalone websites for the smaller meetings that medical societies run around their main congress, including focus meetings, courses and satellite symposia. Each site has the programme, faculty, venue and registration in one place, and I keep it updated with changes until the meeting takes place.

Case study · 2026

ESSKA Focus Meetings 2027

In 2026 I built six microsites for ESSKA’s focus meetings: six subjects in six cities, from April to October 2027. Each meeting has its own site with programme, faculty, venue and registration, and its own colour and imagery inside one shared system.
